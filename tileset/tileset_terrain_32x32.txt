tileset_terrain_32x32.png - 32x32 tiles, 10 columns x 12 rows
name, column, row (both counted from 0, top-left)
suffixes: nw n ne / w c e / sw s se = the nine parts of a patch on grass
inner_nw inner_ne inner_sw inner_se = concave corners, named by the
quadrant of the tile that is grass (the rest of the tile is the terrain)
path_narrow_<sides> = one-tile-wide path; <sides> lists the sides it joins
(n e s w), e.g. ns = straight up-down, ne = bend, nes = T, dot = no joins
path_join_<side> = edge of the wide path where a narrow path leaves that side
river_<sides> = one-tile-wide water, named the same way as path_narrow_<sides>
river_join_<side> = edge of the pond where a river leaves that side
river_bridge_ew = bridge walked east-west, use in place of river_ns
river_bridge_ns = bridge walked north-south, use in place of river_ew
wall_upper = indoor wall without the skirting board, goes above wall_indoor
